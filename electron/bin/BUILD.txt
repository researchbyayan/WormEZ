Original configure command (retained ffprobe build):
./configure --disable-everything --disable-doc --disable-debug --disable-ffplay
--disable-network --disable-x86asm --disable-bzlib --disable-zlib --disable-iconv
--enable-ffmpeg --enable-ffprobe
--enable-protocol=file
--enable-demuxer=mov,matroska,avi,asf,mpegts,mpegps,mpegvideo,mjpeg
--enable-decoder=h264,hevc,vp8,vp9,mpeg1video,mpeg2video,mpeg4,wmv1,wmv2,wmv3,vc1,mjpeg,prores,rawvideo
--enable-parser=h264,hevc,vp8,vp9,mpeg4video,mpegvideo,vc1,mjpeg
--enable-encoder=h264_videotoolbox,mpeg4 --enable-muxer=mp4,mov
--enable-filter=scale,format,fps --enable-swscale --enable-avfilter
--enable-videotoolbox

Networking is disabled. WormEZ passes only local paths selected or recorded by the
user. Audio is omitted from normalized analysis copies. Conversion first tries
H.264 VideoToolbox, then falls back to FFmpeg's native MPEG-4 encoder in MP4.
